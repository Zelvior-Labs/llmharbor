Privacy Policy.

LLMHarbor is a self-hosted router. The short version: your provider keys stay on your machine, and nothing is sent to Zelvior Labs.

What we hold, and where

Self-hosted by default. When you run LLMHarbor yourself, everything — provider keys, routing state, usage counts — stays on the machine you run it on. Nothing is sent to us.

Encrypted keys. Provider API keys you add to the dashboard are encrypted at rest, and clients only ever receive one unified key scoped to the endpoints you allow.

No hosted service. LLMHarbor has no paid plan and no hosted service, so none of your requests pass through us. A separate paid service exists at freellmapi.co, run by Tashfeen Ahmed, the author of the original freellmapi. It is not part of LLMHarbor, and its own privacy policy applies.

Usage. Token counts are kept per key so you can watch each provider's free-tier cap. These are aggregate counts, not the content of your requests.

Advertising. This website (not the router software) shows ads from third-party networks, including bellnewyork.org and ardance.org. Every ad is labelled “Advertisement” or “Sponsored link” and loads in its own frame. The networks may set cookies or collect data under their own policies, which we do not control. Ads never appear on the Download, Privacy, Terms or Links pages.

Contact. For privacy questions, reach Zelvior Labs through the links on the Links page. This policy may be updated; the latest version is always here.
