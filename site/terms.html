Where does OSSLLMAPI come from?

OSSLLMAPI is a modified version of freellmapi by tashfeenahmed, reworked and maintained by Zelvior Labs. The original project is linked in the repository.

What is the premium subscription?

The free model catalog is delivered as a monthly signed snapshot. A premium subscription at ossllmapi.co ($19 a year, cancel anytime) delivers the live catalog the same day new models, quota changes and compatibility fixes land. The subscription is a paid service with its own checkout terms, shown at purchase.

Are the provider free tiers guaranteed?

No. Provider free tiers, model availability and quota caps are set by each provider and can change or be withdrawn at any time. OSSLLMAPI routes around limits and failovers, but it cannot guarantee a provider's free tier.
