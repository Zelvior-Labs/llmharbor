Privacy. Short, plain, and boring on purpose.

OSSLLMAPI is self-hosted, open-source software. This website does not track you, does not run advertising, and does not sell personal data.

Does this website track visitors?

No. The site runs no analytics, no advertising, no cross-site tracking and no advertising cookies. It is a static site served from Vercel.

What does Vercel log?

Vercel, the hosting provider, may log standard hosting data — request timestamps, IP addresses and request paths — for security and reliability. That data is handled under Vercel's own privacy policy.

What happens to my provider keys?

When you self-host OSSLLMAPI, your provider keys are stored encrypted at rest on your own machine. Clients only ever see the single unified API key you mint in the dashboard, and per-key token budgets keep every provider under its free-tier cap.

Does OSSLLMAPI send my prompts anywhere?

Only to the LLM provider you chose for that request, as the request requires. OSSLLMAPI itself does not log your prompts or completions; the router's job is to move them to the provider and count the tokens.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. OSSLLMAPI runs on your machine or your own server. Delete the data directory and the keys, and everything is gone. Nothing about your use is stored by this website.

Who do I contact?

Zelvior Labs. The fastest routes are the project's GitHub repository and the Discord server, both linked on the home page.
