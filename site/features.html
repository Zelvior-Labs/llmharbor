The router does the work.

Four jobs turn a pile of free-tier keys into one dependable endpoint: picking, failing over, hiding keys and counting tokens.

Picks the best model

Every request is routed to the fastest model that can serve it, chosen from your ordered fallback chain.

Falls over on rate limits

When a provider throttles or hits its cap, the next provider takes the request before your caller ever sees it.
